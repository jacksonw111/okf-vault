#观察 MacRumor分析师通过挖掘iOS 27 Beta 5更新代码，发现苹果正在为国行Apple Intelligence苹果智能做准备。

代码显示，“Apple Intelligence专为隐私而设计。为遵守相关法律法规，中国地区的Apple Intelligence使用由本地公司提供的安全机制。您的请求均在设备上处理，不会发送给Apple或安全机制提供商。根据法律要求，Apple将收集匿名化的安全结果，并以汇总形式分享。安全机制将自动下载并更新。”

> 引用内容

> iOS 27 beta 5 preps support for Apple Intelligence in China
> 
> "Apple Intelligence is built for privacy. To comply with laws and regulations, Apple Intelligence in China uses a safety mechanism provided by a local company. Your requests are processed on-device and not sent to Apple or the safety mechanism provider. As required by law, Apple will collect anonymized safety results and share them in aggregate. The safety mechanism will download and update automatically."
